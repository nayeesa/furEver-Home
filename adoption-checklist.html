Adoption Checklist

  • Research your new pet’s needs: Learn about their dietary, exercise, and social needs to ensure you can provide the right environment.
  • Prepare your home: Create a safe and comfortable space for your new pet, including bedding, toys, and food/water bowls.
  • Gather supplies: Stock up on pet essentials such as food, grooming tools, a leash or carrier, and cleaning supplies.
  • Plan your schedule: Be ready to dedicate time for training, vet visits, and bonding with your new pet.
  • Arrange veterinary care: Schedule a check-up and vaccinations with a trusted veterinarian soon after adoption.
  • Consider pet insurance: Research insurance plans to help manage unexpected health costs.
  • Set house rules: Decide on boundaries and routines to help your pet adjust smoothly to their new home.
  • Stay patient and loving: Remember that every pet adjusts at their own pace—provide lots of love and support!
