At furEver Home, we’re passionate about connecting loving families with rescued cats and dogs. Our mission is to create happy endings through a smooth and caring adoption experience. We believe every pet deserves a family, and we’re here to make that happen by providing a trusted, user‑friendly platform for pet adoption.

Important Notice: FurEver Home is strictly an adoption platform and is not intended for selling pets. Our purpose is to help pets find loving, caring homes where they can thrive, and we ask all users to respect this mission by refraining from using the site for commercial pet trading.

Who We Are

furEver Home connects rescued cats and dogs with loving families through a simple, transparent process.

Why Us

  • Trusted shelter partners

How It Works

  • Browse our trusted shelter partners’ available pets.
  • Use our easy online application to start the adoption process.
  • Meet your potential new family member and finalize the adoption.
  • Receive ongoing support and resources after adoption.
